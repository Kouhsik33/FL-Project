# Final technical report

**Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Medical Diagnosis.** Simulated research prototype; final validation completed 2026-10-21.

> All institutions are simulated on one computer using public, de-identified UCI datasets. No clinical validation was performed. Nothing here supports clinical use.

Full tables: `results/report/tables.md` (generated by `python -m fedmed.experiments.report`). Per-run raw data: `results/runs/<run_id>/summary.json`. Background: [technical_research.md](technical_research.md), [dataset_assessment.md](dataset_assessment.md), [architecture.md](architecture.md).

---

## 1. Methodology

**Task.** Binary classification. WDBC (primary): malignant = positive, 569 records, 30 features. UCI Heart Disease (secondary): disease present (`num > 0`) = positive, 920 records from 4 real sites, 16 features after preprocessing. The decision threshold is fixed at 0.5. Precision, recall and F1 are computed for the positive class (binary averaging).

**Splits and leakage controls.**
- One stratified 60/20/20 train/val/test split per dataset (`split_seed = 0`). The test sets have 114 records (WDBC) and 184 (Heart Disease), and are shared by every run.
- Imputation and standardisation statistics come from client training partitions only.
- Hyper-parameters (SGD, lr 0.005, momentum 0.9, batch 16, 20 epochs) were chosen on the validation split by `fedmed.experiments.tune`. The test set was used only for final reporting.

**Model.** MLP 30→32→16→1 (ReLU), 1,537 parameters for WDBC, `BCEWithLogitsLoss`.

**Compared settings.**
- *Centralised*: pooled training split, 20 epochs.
- *Local-only*: each institution trains alone for 20 epochs. The reported value is the mean of the local models on the global test set.
- *FedAvg*: Flower 1.40 simulation, sample-weighted FedAvg, 1 local epoch per round, 20 rounds. This is the pre-registered matched epoch budget. Group H adds 50 and 100 rounds; it was pre-registered before any group-H result existed.

**Seeds.** 5 seeds (0–4) for groups A–D and G; 3 seeds (0–2) for F and H. The seed controls model initialisation, batching, DP noise, the client partition, and the dropout schedule. Values are mean ± sample standard deviation over seeds.

**Experiment inventory.** 199 runs, all completed: 184 in the main suite (groups A–G) plus 15 in group H. There were 0 failed runs (`results/suite_index.jsonl`).

## 2. Results

### 2.1 Centralised vs local-only vs FedAvg (group A, IID, WDBC)

| Setting | Accuracy | Recall | F1 | ROC-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Centralised | 0.965 ± 0.000 | 0.952 ± 0.000 | 0.952 ± 0.000 | 0.991 ± 0.001 |
| Local-only k3 / k5 / k10 | 0.938 / 0.929 / 0.864 | 0.921 / 0.874 / 0.653 | 0.917 / 0.901 / 0.752 | 0.983 / 0.973 / 0.961 |
| FedAvg 20 rounds, k3 | 0.926 ± 0.020 | 0.814 ± 0.043 | 0.890 ± 0.031 | 0.969 ± 0.004 |
| FedAvg 20 rounds, k5 | 0.777 ± 0.119 | 0.452 ± 0.392 | 0.513 ± 0.364 | 0.951 ± 0.008 |
| FedAvg 20 rounds, k10 | 0.595 ± 0.062 | 0.405 ± 0.522 | 0.270 ± 0.306 | 0.902 ± 0.035 |

**Finding (negative, H1 not supported at 20 rounds).** Under the pre-registered 20-round budget, FedAvg was worse than both centralised and local-only training, and got worse as the number of clients grew. The diagnosis, made on the **validation** set and training logs and never on test data:
- At round 20, validation log loss is still 0.626 (k5) and 0.660 (k10), versus 0.084 for centralised. It is still decreasing.
- The global weights moved only 6% (relative L2) from initialisation.
- Each client takes only 8 / 5 / 3 SGD steps per round (k3 / k5 / k10), and the momentum-0.9 optimiser state is reset every round. Local-only training, by contrast, keeps its momentum over all 20 epochs.

The models rank cases reasonably (AUC 0.90–0.95), but their probabilities stay near the prior. Whether recall is 0.02 or 0.88 then depends mostly on the seed. This is a property of the experimental budget, not an implementation error: aggregation is unit-tested, and SecAgg+ reproduces plain FedAvg (§2.5).

### 2.3 Data heterogeneity (group B, k5, 20 rounds)

| Partition | FedAvg F1 | Local-only F1 | FedAvg client-F1 gap | Local client-F1 gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IID (group A) | 0.513 ± 0.364 | 0.901 ± 0.018 | — | — |
| Dirichlet α=1.0 | 0.471 ± 0.418 | 0.594 ± 0.125 | 0.340 ± 0.191 | 0.683 ± 0.416 |
| Dirichlet α=0.3 | 0.508 ± 0.465 | 0.345 ± 0.195 | 0.234 ± 0.425 | 0.620 ± 0.492 |
| Dirichlet α=0.1 | 0.385 ± 0.506 | 0.401 ± 0.122 | 0.234 ± 0.431 | 0.264 ± 0.413 |
| Quantity skew α=0.5 | 0.848 ± 0.082 | 0.708 ± 0.175 | 0.342 ± 0.278 | 0.674 ± 0.365 |

- Under label skew, isolated institutions fail badly. Local-only F1 drops from 0.901 (IID) to 0.35–0.59, because a client that sees mostly one class predicts mostly that class.
- At 20 rounds FedAvg is too under-trained (§2.1) to support a clean IID-vs-non-IID ranking, so **H2 is inconclusive** at that budget. The variance across seeds exceeds the differences between partitions.
- What *is* consistent: the per-client F1 gap (best minus worst institution on its own test shard) is smaller under FedAvg than under local-only training in every skewed partition.
- Per-client shards hold only 11–38 records, so individual gaps are noisy. This is disparity across simulated institutions, not evidence about demographic or clinical fairness.

### 2.4 Differential privacy (group C; record-level DP-SGD, δ = 1e-5)

| σ | Centralised ε | Centralised F1 | FedAvg k5 ε (max client) | FedAvg k5 F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no DP | — | 0.952 ± 0.000 | — | 0.513 ± 0.364 |
| 0.6 | 23.15 | 0.943 ± 0.018 | 42.77 | 0.406 ± 0.423 |
| 1.0 | 7.02 | 0.941 ± 0.015 | 15.97 | 0.409 ± 0.426 |
| 1.5 | 3.49 | 0.936 ± 0.018 | 8.28 | 0.406 ± 0.422 |
| 2.0 | 2.33 | 0.929 ± 0.015 | 5.50 | 0.410 ± 0.428 |
| 3.0 | 1.40 | 0.923 ± 0.010 | 3.25 | 0.405 ± 0.420 |

- **Centralised (converged): H3 supported.** F1 decreases monotonically as ε decreases, from 0.952 without DP to 0.923 at ε = 1.40. That is a modest cost on this easy task.
- **Federated at 20 rounds: inconclusive.** Utility is flat across a 13× range of ε. The final weights *do* differ from the non-DP model (relative L2 2.8% at σ = 0.6, 13.8% at σ = 3.0), but the barely trained model leaves the thresholded metrics dominated by the seed.
- With 50 rounds (group H), FedAvg + DP at σ = 1.0 reached F1 0.880 ± 0.031, versus 0.911 ± 0.028 without DP, at a cost of **ε = 26.70**. More rounds mean more noisy steps, so a higher ε.
- **Why ε is large in FL.** Each institution holds about 68 records, so the Poisson sampling rate is q = 16/68 ≈ 0.2, compared with ≈ 0.05 for the pooled data. Small cohorts get little privacy amplification from subsampling. For the same σ, the federated ε is 1.85–2.37× the centralised one.
- Cost: DP-SGD roughly doubled client training time (total 2.06 s vs 1.12 s per k5 run).

### 2.5 Secure aggregation (group F, 3 seeds) and communication

| Setting | Messages per run | Bytes per aggregated round | FL wall time (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FedAvg k5 | 100 | 72,285 | 6.0 |
| FedAvg + SecAgg+ k5 | 400 | 146,732 (2.03×) | 16.5 |
| FedAvg k10 | 200 | 144,560 | 7.2 |
| FedAvg + SecAgg+ k10 | 800 | 382,859 (2.65×) | 27.5 |

- **Utility.** Seed for seed, SecAgg+ reproduced plain FedAvg. The thresholded metrics were identical and AUC differed only in the fourth decimal; the final-weight difference was 0.04% relative L2, from quantisation. An end-to-end test also checks agreement within 1e-3 per parameter under DP and mid-round crashes. **H5 supported.**
- **Traffic by stage** (k5, seed 0, 20 rounds):

  | Stage | Bytes |
  |---|---|
  | setup | 67,800 |
  | share keys | 494,520 |
  | masked vectors | 2,321,620 |
  | unmask | 51,100 |
  | plaintext training, for comparison | 1,445,700 |

  Masked vectors are int64 (8 bytes per parameter, versus 4 for float32). Key sharing grows faster than linearly with the number of clients: 494,520 bytes at k5 versus 2,080,120 at k10.
- The model payload is 6,916 bytes per transfer. All sizes are protobuf-serialised message content, excluding gRPC/TLS/TCP framing. Network time was **not** measured, because everything runs in-process. Wall times include Ray scheduling overhead.

| Setting | F1 | Rounds aggregated (of 20) |
|---|---|---|
| k5, no dropout | 0.513 ± 0.364 | 20 |
| k5, 20% unavailable | 0.516 ± 0.366 | 20 ± 0 |

- Dropout was applied as configured. For example, seed 0 of the k5 runs had 24 (20% rate) and 60 (50% rate) unavailable client-rounds.

## 3. Privacy assumptions (what the numbers do and do not mean)

- **FL alone** keeps records inside each client's partition, but model updates can still leak information. It is not a privacy guarantee.
- **ε values** are RDP-accountant bounds for **record-level** DP at each institution: add/remove one record, Poisson sampling, δ = 1e-5. They are composed over every local step in every round that institution trained in, and we report the maximum over institutions.
  - They are **not** client-level guarantees.
  - They do **not** cover the preprocessing sufficient statistics or the exact sample counts sent to the server.
  - Opacus `secure_mode` was off, which is acceptable for simulation but not for deployment.
- **SecAgg+** (Flower 1.40) hides individual parameter vectors from an honest-but-curious server, provided fewer than the threshold of parties collude. The server still sees `num_examples`, fit metrics, participation and the aggregate. We enforce at least 3 participants. The in-process simulation has no real trust boundary and no TLS.
- **DP + SecAgg together** do not eliminate every attack. Poisoning, malicious-server variants and inference from the released model (bounded only by ε) remain.

## 5. Issues found and fixed during validation

1. **F1 under-reporting bug** (`evaluation/metrics.py`).
   - *Symptom:* F1 was stored as undefined (`None`) when a model predicted no positives on a set that contained positives. Seed means then silently skipped exactly the worst runs; for example, Dirichlet α = 0.3 at 20 rounds showed F1 0.856, but the correct value is 0.571.
   - *Fix:* F1 = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN), which is 0 in that case. Regression test added.
   - *Recovery:* the original 199 summaries were archived (`results/archive/summaries_before_f1_fix.tar.gz`, SHA-256 `057af454…`). F1 was then recomputed exactly from the stored confusion counts by `scripts/recompute_f1.py`, which is idempotent. 94 runs were affected; no model was retrained and no setting changed. Precision remains genuinely undefined in that case, and tables mark it with †(k/n).
2. **Dashboard DP page pooled different experiments** that shared a σ. For example, its "federated σ = 1.0" point showed F1 0.592 at ε 17.98, mixing four experiments; the true sweep point is F1 0.409 at ε 15.97. Fixed: the page now plots the group-C sweep only and lists other DP runs separately. Regression test added.
3. **Dashboard cache never invalidated:** the underscore-prefixed `_stamp` argument was excluded from Streamlit's cache key. Renamed to `stamp`.
4. **`tune` crashed in a fresh clone** with no `results/` directory (`FileNotFoundError`). Fixed in `tune` and `suite`. Regression test added, and both commands were re-verified as first commands in the fresh environment.

The raw logs `server_log.json` and `client_log.jsonl` were left untouched, so their per-round F1 values still use the old definition. `summary.json` and everything derived from it use the corrected values.

**Operational note.** During the fresh-environment check, one run's process time was inflated to 65,318 s by a system sleep: the macOS power log shows a 65,290 s "Clamshell Sleep". That run's measured simulation time (23.3 s) is unaffected. None of the 199 reported runs show such a gap: the longest took 72.5 s (`fedavg_secagg_k10`, seed 2).

## 6. Limitations

- Simulated institutions on one machine. The datasets are small and historical, and their partitions are artificial (except the Heart Disease sites). Per-client shards hold 11–61 records.
- The main comparison used a fixed, pre-registered budget whose hyper-parameters were tuned for centralised training. FedAvg-specific tuning was not done, so the 20-round FedAvg results understate what FedAvg can reach; group H shows this.
- Only 3 seeds in groups F and H. Wide confidence intervals throughout. No significance testing.
- DP is record-level only, with `secure_mode` off. Preprocessing statistics and sample counts are outside the guarantee.
- SecAgg+ runs in-process: no TLS, no authentication, and Flower still reveals metadata in plaintext.
- The coordinator evaluates on held-out data as an oracle; federated evaluation was not implemented. Network time is unmeasured.

## 7. Future work

Ordered by expected value:
1. Tune FL hyper-parameters (learning rate, local epochs, server-side momentum / FedAvgM, rounds) on validation data only, then re-run groups A–D with that pre-registered configuration.
2. Run more seeds and report confidence intervals or paired tests.
3. Client-level DP via Flower's `DifferentialPrivacyClientSideFixedClipping`, and DP-protected preprocessing statistics.
4. Federated evaluation, so the coordinator never holds evaluation data.
5. Deployment-mode Flower (SuperLink/SuperNodes in separate processes or machines, TLS, node authentication) to measure real network time.
6. Robust aggregation against malicious clients, and evaluation of membership-inference risk with and without DP.
